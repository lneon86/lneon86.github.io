FREQUENTLY ASKED

Mr.Stonk is an NFT collection connected to the $STONK token ecosystem, combining staking, farming, and a decentralized jackpot inspired by the stock market.

Each Mr.Stonk NFT costs 25,000 $STONK to mint.

Stake your Mr.Stonk NFT to activate its daily farming rate and become eligible for jackpot spins.

Each staked NFT generates 1,000 $STONK per day in daily farm rewards.

Every staked NFT receives one jackpot spin every 24 hours.

Spin the three-row machine — matching three Mr.Stonk symbols across all rows wins the current jackpot pool.

Winners are selected through a decentralized randomization mechanism among eligible staked NFTs.

Jackpot rewards rotate between $STONK and stock-themed reward tickers, and may vary each round.

Use the CLAIM REWARD button on the Farm dashboard or the jackpot win screen once eligible.
